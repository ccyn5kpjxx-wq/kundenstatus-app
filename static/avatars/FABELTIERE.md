# Märchenhafte Werkstatt-Avatare

Eigene fiktive Figuren, mit dem eingebauten Bildgenerierungswerkzeug erzeugt.
Alle Dateien haben vier transparente Frames: Ruhe, Sprechmund A, Sprechmund O, Blinzeln.
Animation folgt dem Gesprächszustand; keine phonemgenaue Videoanimation.

## Verwendete Prompts

### Drache

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production sprite sheet for a speaking avatar in a German car workshop assistant. Create ONE square transparent PNG with exactly FOUR equal square frames in a strict 2x2 grid, no gaps or dividers. Same friendly SMALL DRAGON character bust in every frame, directly facing camera. Subtle fairytale, approachable and capable, not a monster, not childish baby proportions. Soft premium 3D animated illustration, sage-teal scales, warm golden eyes, small cream horns, cream throat, tiny hints of folded wings behind shoulders, rounded snout with a clearly visible mouth. No costume, no objects, no flames. Bust of head and upper body filling each tile, hair/horns and wings fully inside each square, torso ends at each square bottom edge. Exactly identical scale, pose, position and lighting in all FOUR frames. Top LEFT eyes OPEN and mouth CLOSED gentle smile. Top RIGHT eyes OPEN, small open A speaking mouth. Bottom LEFT eyes OPEN, small round O speaking mouth. Bottom RIGHT eyes CLOSED blinking, same closed-mouth gentle smile. Align all facial features across the four tiles perfectly; only eyelids and mouth move. Genuinely transparent background in all tiles, clean edges, warm soft studio lighting and dimensional shading. No text, logo, labels, watermark, dividers, props or additional characters.
